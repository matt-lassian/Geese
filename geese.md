## Migration Habits of Canadian Geese

Canadian geese are well-known for their distinctive V-formation flights during migration. Here are some interesting facts about their migration patterns:

- They typically migrate twice a year: southward in fall and northward in spring
- Migration can cover distances of 2,000 to 3,000 miles each way
- They fly at altitudes of 2,000 to 8,000 feet, but can reach heights of up to 29,000 feet
- The V-formation helps them conserve energy, with birds taking turns leading the formation
- They navigate using the sun, stars, and Earth's magnetic field
- Migration typically occurs during daylight hours
- They often return to the same nesting and wintering grounds year after year

During migration, they make frequent stops at bodies of water to rest and feed, often staying at these stopover points for several days before continuing their journey.
