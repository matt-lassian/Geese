## What Do Ducks Eat?

Ducks are omnivorous birds that enjoy a varied diet including:
- Aquatic plants and algae
- Seeds and grains
- Small fish and fish eggs
- Insects and small invertebrates
- Crustaceans and mollusks
- Water plants and grasses

In urban areas, ducks may also eat bread, though it's not the most nutritious option for them. It's better to feed them:
- Frozen peas or corn (thawed)
- Oats
- Chopped lettuce
- Duck pellets
